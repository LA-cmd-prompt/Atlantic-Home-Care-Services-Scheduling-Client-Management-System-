# Atlantic-Home-Care-Services-Scheduling-Client-Management-System-

**Client Type:** Home Care Agency (Healthcare)  
**Role:** Business Analyst (Process & Requirements lead)  
**Focus:** Collaborative requirements gathering, feasible documentation, and stakeholder engagement

---

## Project Painpoint

A home care agency was managing all scheduling, client visits, and caregiver assignments manually through spreadsheets and phone calls. This caused frequent double-bookings, missed visits, and poor visibility for both staff and leadership.

I led the business analysis work from start to finish by facilitating workshops, mapping processes, and translating stakeholder needs into clear, testable requirements that the development team could actually build.

---

## Approach & Value Add

I used a **collaborative and inclusive** style because many frontline staff (nurses and schedulers) struggled to articulate their needs in traditional meetings. I combined simple techniques like silent brainstorming with sticky notes and “Day in the Life” walkthroughs to make everyone feel comfortable contributing.

At the same time, I applied a **developer lens** from the beginning by ensuring requirements were realistic, had clear acceptance criteria, and considered integration, performance, and maintainability.



---

## Key Results & Impact

- **42% reduction** in scheduling conflicts within the first 3 months of go-live
- **35% decrease** in time spent on daily scheduling (from ~4 hours to under 2.5 hours)
- **28% improvement** in caregiver utilization rate
- **19% reduction** in missed or rescheduled visits
- **Significantly higher** staff satisfaction with the new process (qualitative feedback from 87% of schedulers and nurses)

These results were achieved by focusing on **collaborative workshops** and writing **feasible, well-defined requirements** from the start.
